Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SocietyHub collects residential administration data including name, email address, flat assignment, maintenance dues, visitor check-in logs, and complaint ticket histories strictly for operating housing society services.

2. How Information is Used

Your data is used to verify residential access, process maintenance payments, dispatch gate visitor notifications, and provide executive analytics to elected society committee members.

3. Data Security & Storage

All credentials and tokens are secured via Firebase Authentication and server-side JWT encryption. We do not sell or monetize personal residential data.

4. Contact Us

For privacy requests or account data deletion, contact your Society Administrator or email support at privacy@societyhub.app.
